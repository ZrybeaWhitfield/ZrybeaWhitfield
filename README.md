### Hello, my name is Zrybea!

I'm a software engineer who spends most of my professional time in the backend these days, usually somewhere between Java, cloud infrastructure, APIs, production systems, and figuring out why something is behaving differently than expected.

Outside of my day job, I like building things for myself, tinkering with new ideas, mentoring and teaching when I get the chance, and occasionally talking about tech in front of other people.

When I am nowhere near a code editor, you can usually find me playing video games, working out, reading, or eating an unreasonable amount of cheese.
