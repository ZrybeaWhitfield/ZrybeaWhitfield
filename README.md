## Hello, my name is Zrybea!

I'm a software engineer who spends most of my time on backend development these days, usually somewhere between Java, APIs, cloud infrastructure, production systems, and figuring out why something is behaving differently than expected. AI has also become a regular part of how I work, especially for research, planning, testing, and getting through the parts of development that benefit from a second set of eyes.

JavaScript still has a special place in my heart though. It was where I really learned how to build things, and it is still usually what I reach for when I want to play around, make something small, or build something just because I can. Java is where things tend to get a little more serious 😏.

Before I was a developer, I worked in behavioral health and served as a medic in the United States Army Reserve. I took a pretty winding road into software engineering, so I am always happy to talk about getting into tech, learning in public, or figuring things out as you go.


## What I work with

I do most of my backend work in **Java with Quarkus**, and I still use **Python** and **JavaScript/Node.js** pretty regularly depending on what I’m building.

On the cloud side, I’ve worked with both **GCP and AWS**, and most of my database work these days is in **MySQL and Cassandra**. I also spend a lot of time around APIs, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, observability, logs, metrics, and all the other less glamorous things that come with keeping services running.

## Outside of code

Outside of my day job, I like building for myself, tinkering with new ideas, mentoring and teaching when I get the chance, and finding more opportunities to talk about tech in front of other people.

A lot of what I enjoy about tech happens in community with other people. I currently serve on the board of [Resilient Coders](https://www.resilientcoders.org/), the organization that helped me make my own transition into software engineering.

When I am nowhere near a code editor, you can usually find me playing video games, working out, reading, or eating an unreasonable amount of cheese.
